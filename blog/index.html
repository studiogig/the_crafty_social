Why I created The Crafty Club Social

Surgery, crochet, wine, and learning to sit still. The real story behind why this exists.

February 2026 · Diya
· · · · · · ·

Have you got a granny craft story to share?

We'd love to hear it

The ambition for this blog is simple: it’s just a place to share if you want to, who knows, it might help someone, make them smile, or just be fun to read about the madness that takes over when you get into buying yarns!

Big stories. Small stories. The messy bits. The quiet wins. The things you don’t usually say out loud.

You don’t need to be a writer. You don’t need perfect grammar. You don’t need a dramatic backstory.

All stories will be anonymous, feel free to make up a crafty name. It’s a small few villages, I don’t want anyone to feel exposed or weird.

If you’d like to share, DM me. Photos of any creations are very, very welcomed too.

Let’s create a little corner of the internet where we can put things down for a while and know someone else is holding space too.
